Professional Summary

Microbiome scientist and evolutionary ecologist with over a decade of research experience investigating host–microbe interactions across model organisms and clinical cohorts. My postdoctoral training bridges integrative biology and invertebrate genetics at UC Berkeley (Caenorhabditis elegans niche construction and aging) with immunology, translational pharmacology, and computational metagenomics at UCSF (mouse arthritis models, pharmacomicrobiomics, and functional genomics), complemented by a doctoral degree from NCCS Pune and a Fulbright-Nehru Fellowship at Mayo Clinic.

My research program investigates how hosts construct their microbial environments and how these microbial legacies are transmitted across generations to influence offspring fitness, development, and therapeutic drug response. Combining invertebrate genetic tractability, gnotobiotic mammalian models, and multi-cohort clinical metagenomics, I aim to establish an independent research group dissecting the ecological inheritance of host-constructed microbiomes and their translational consequences.

Research Interests

Dissecting how hosts construct their microbial environments and how microbial legacies shape health, evolution, and drug response

Rahul Bodkhe, Ph.D. — Research Program

Combining invertebrate genetic tractability (Caenorhabditis elegans), gnotobiotic mammalian models of autoimmunity, and multi-cohort clinical metagenomics to dissect the ecological inheritance of host-constructed microbiomes and their translational consequences for drug response.

Pillar I • Evolutionary Ecology

Ecological Inheritance & Niche Construction

Elucidating the behavioral and genetic drivers of host-constructed microbial environments, and evaluating how persistent microbial legacies alter community assembly and offspring life-history traits across generations in Caenorhabditis elegans.

Pillar II • Mammalian Models

Intergenerational Microbiome Transmission in Mammals

Delineating maternal vs. environmentally mediated transmission routes (bedding, feces, nesting) of host-shaped microbial communities and determining their causal influence on inflammatory arthritis progression and methotrexate therapy response.

Pillar III • Translational Metagenomics

Pharmacomicrobiomics & Functional Metagenomics

Multi-cohort metagenomic and resistome profiling to discover predictive biomarkers of treatment response in autoimmune disease, and identifying bacterial functional genes targeted by drugs using high-throughput functional genomics (TnSeq).

Core Technical Expertise

Invertebrate Genetics & Microbial Ecology

C. elegans culture, colonization kinetics, boom-to-bust population dynamics, defined gnotobiotic communities (CeMbio), behavioral and developmental mutants (eat-2, aex-1, daf-22), and Compost Microbial Extract (CME) preparation.

Mammalian Immunology & In Vivo Models

Zymosan-induced SKG arthritis and humanized mouse models, gnotobiotic colonizations, reciprocal bedding transfers, cross-fostering, fecal microbiota transplantation (FMT), antibiotic-mediated depletion, clinical arthritis scoring, and T-cell assays.

Genomics Infrastructure & Wet-Lab NGS

End-to-end wet-lab sequencing infrastructure establishment, low-input DNA extraction protocols, shotgun metagenomic and 16S rRNA amplicon library preparation, Bacteroides thetaiotaomicron TnSeq functional screening, and anaerobic bacterial isolation.

Bioinformatics & Computational Metagenomics

Full pipeline development and management for shotgun metagenomics (taxonomic profiling, functional annotation, resistome quantification), 16S amplicon analysis, statistical modeling in R (linear mixed-effects models, compositionally aware multivariate statistics), and HPC computing.
